Pokhara Engineering College

Pokhara College of Management (PCM) is one of Pokhara's leading private colleges affiliated with Pokhara University, offering programs in BBA, BBA-Finance, and BCSIT. Known for its experienced faculty, modern facilities, and strong focus on producing industry-ready graduates.

PCM is a premier management college in Western Nepal, located at Gyanmarg, Nadipur, Pokhara-3, Kaski. Since its establishment in 2002, it has been committed to providing affordable quality education under the motto: Enter to Learn — Go Forth to Serve.
