Apex College

Apex College is one of Kathmandu's leading private colleges affiliated with Pokhara University, offering programs in BBA, BCSIT, and more. Known for its modern campus, experienced faculty, and strong industry connections.

Apex College is a premier business school in Nepal, primarily located on Devkota Sadak in Mid-Baneshwor, Kathmandu.
