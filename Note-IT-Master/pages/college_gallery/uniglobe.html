Uniglobe College

Uniglobe College is one of Kathmandu's prominent private colleges affiliated with Pokhara University, offering programs in BBA, BBA-Finance, BCSIT, MBA, and MBA-Finance. Known for its academically experienced faculty, modern facilities, and a strong vision for producing globally competitive graduates.

Uniglobe College is a leading management and IT institution in Nepal, located at Kalimarga, New Baneshwor, Kathmandu. Established in 2009, it has been dedicated to delivering affordable, quality education and has since grown into one of the most recognized colleges under Pokhara University.
